# CloseLens · 收盘透镜

一次事实采集，一份完整研究，一次可核验交付。

## Five stages

1. **Scope and time.** Identify the requested securities, output and actual exchange session. Reuse an explicitly confirmed watchlist; never treat it as holdings. For a full close report, cover every item (including a 52-item list when configured), with 3–5 deeper cases. A single-company request stays single-company. Do not invent a list.
2. **Evidence once.** Prefer existing authorized market sources and free issuer, SEC and exchange disclosures. Preserve links, publication/reporting dates, retrieval timestamps, currency, session and adjustment basis. Reuse the same factual packet across methods. Reject conflicting identities, stale sessions, impossible OHLC and incomplete bars; leave gaps rather than substituting prior or after-hours prices.
3. **Research each item.** Explain observed performance, relevant evidence, conditional interpretation, risks and missing facts. For companies, connect business drivers to revenue, margins, cash flow, balance sheet, dilution and valuation assumptions. For ETFs, identify the actual underlying exposure, leverage/reset and path risks. Do not substitute fee detail for exposure analysis. Distinguish next-session conditions from 1–4-week conditions and invalidation signals.
4. **Model synthesis.** If authorized models are available, run the current factual packet and retain their original outputs and status. Compare agreement, disagreement and complementary evidence. Shared data or a shared underlying model is not independent confirmation. When a model fails, preserve partial output and let the current assistant complete an explicitly attributed synthesis; never claim the failed platform succeeded. Do not repeatedly retry access denial or buy replacement services.
5. **Validate and deliver.** Check complete unique coverage, security identity, current session, source dates, meaningful prose and factual blockers. Produce one final report. Email only when the recipient and task are explicitly authorized; use [delivery.md](references/delivery.md) before sending. Claim completion only after the requested artifact and delivery evidence exist.

## Report contract

Start with 3–5 takeaways. Include each requested security, 3–5 deep cases for a full watchlist report, actual workflow/model results, attributed comparison, next session, 1–4 weeks, risks, gaps and sources. Name-only rows and repeated boilerplate do not count as substantive coverage. Disclose old filings as dated references. Do not invent consensus, probabilities, targets, indicators or return claims.

For a general daily macro brief, include relevant political/economic developments, market/sector changes, supply-chain evidence, catalysts and prior-thesis review. If oil futures are requested, state the exact contract, currency/unit and settlement-versus-live basis. Keep daily briefs and exchange-close reports separately deduplicated.

## Keep it simple

- One confirmed identity map, one dated evidence packet, one durable run state, one final report. Platforms are optional components, not a mandatory checklist.
- Reuse completed stages only when their source/content fingerprints still match. Changed facts require affected conclusions to be rechecked.
- Distinguish collection, rule analysis, model research, failed and not-run states. A connection check is not a research result.
- Call only components that add needed evidence or a distinct method. Do not reload all historical logs or rerun successful models for appearance.
- Do not trade, inspect personal holdings, copy credentials, buy services or change schedules without authorization. This skill does not itself create a scheduler or provide external service access.
- Preserve completeness and truthfulness while shortening workflow overhead. It cannot guarantee investment outcomes or external availability.
